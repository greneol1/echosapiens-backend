
Olivier Grenet is a senior pharmaceutical quality and AI governance leader with more than 20 years of experience in biomedical research quality, compliance, and data integrity. Based in Switzerland and working at Novartis, he has developed expertise in global research quality systems, AI-assisted compliance frameworks, and ethical governance of emerging technologies.

He is recognized for promoting the concept “Quality IN – Intelligence OUT,” emphasizing that reliable data and scientific rigor are the foundations of trustworthy artificial intelligence. Olivier has contributed to international discussions on AI governance, digital sovereignty, and responsible innovation, including participation in United Nations related initiatives and global AI readiness discussions.

His work bridges science, technology, and philosophy, particularly regarding the impact of artificial intelligence on human evolution, cognition, and society. Through the EchoSapiens concept, he explores a new generation of interactive publishing where readers can continue dialogue with authors and historical thinkers beyond the last page of a book.

Professional themes:
- Research Quality and Data Integrity
- AI Governance and Ethical AI
- Compliance by Design
- Biomedical Research Oversight
- Human Evolution and Artificial Intelligence
- Interactive Publishing and Digital Twins
